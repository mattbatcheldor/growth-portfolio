Alex Gilbert — An Adoption Story — Testing the YouTube Halo Effect

Paid discovery + organic storytelling + applied AI

The hard problem: could strategic paid promotion of one YouTube Short create sustained discovery across a ten‑year creator channel of 370+ videos?
159.2KChannel views / 28 days
+282%More views than usual
19.4KWatch hours
+953New subscribers
$5,000Self‑funded test budget
Daily channel views (000s)
A single, self‑funded promotion test activated growth channel‑wide, not just on the promoted Short.

What I did

Earned the creator's trust, personally funded the test, designed audience and keyword targeting, connected the Short to the long‑form story, and measured channel‑wide effects across views, watch time and subscribers.

Applied AI extension

Built ShortsHalo in Google AI Studio — turning the campaign learning and ten years of channel data into a decision‑support tool for organic analysis, promotion scenarios and future content planning.

I saw a massive ripple effect across all my top‑performing videos... thousands more organic views and new subscribers every single day, from the USA, New Zealand, Australia and Canada — exactly the audience I am aiming to reach.

Alex Gilbert — Creator, An Adoption Story
Industry view “The use of both Shorts and long‑form video, from both a paid and organic perspective, showcases how the new breed of creators can drive real success on YouTube.” — Reuben Halper, Head of YouTube, AU/NZ
Targeting signal Adoption & foster‑care and charitable‑donor audiences delivered the strongest engagement at the most efficient cost — and the "YouTube Promoted" source alone drove 9x the views of the next‑best keyword, confirming paid promotion (not organic search) triggered the initial discovery.

What came next

The audience data, keyword performance and ten years of channel history from this experiment became the training ground for ShortsHalo — the case studies are sequential, not parallel: the campaign came first, the AI tool was built to systematise what it taught me.
